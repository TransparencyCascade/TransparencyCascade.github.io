    Moving around

    Drag to move the map. Shift-drag, or drag with two fingers, to tilt and turn. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a county to select it; shift-click or long-press to add. Keys: ← → elections, 1–4 lenses, V next view, Space play, Esc clear, / find, T table.

    Notes and sources

    Data and licences

    Every dataset the page draws on, with the terms it is published under and the credit it asks for. Licences were read from each source's own page on 2026-11-05. Our own compiled files carry the sources' terms where they have any.

    MIT Election Data and Science Lab
    County and state election returns, 1976-2024 (president, Senate, governor; precinct files summed to counties). CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), as each Harvard Dataverse record states (doi:10.7910/DVN/VOQCHQ, 42MVDX, PEJ5QU, NVQYMG, NT66Z3, UYQIEP and the other precinct-return records of the series). No credit is required; the lab asks to be cited: MIT Election Data and Science Lab, Harvard Dataverse.
    U.S. Census Bureau
    Population counts, apportionment, citizen voting-age population (ACS), tract population centres for the dot map, and the county outlines (via us-atlas, ISC licence). A work of the U.S. Government, not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. 105). Credit: "U.S. Census Bureau", as its citation guidance asks.
    Federal Election Commission
    Campaign money (bulk files) and certified results. A work of the U.S. Government (17 U.S.C. 105). One restriction: information copied from FEC reports may not be sold or used to solicit contributions or for commercial purposes (52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)). The page shows these figures for reporting and analysis.
    U.S. Election Assistance Commission
    Election Administration and Voting Survey (registration, removals, rejected ballots; 2016-2024). A work of the U.S. Government (17 U.S.C. 105); eac.gov states no further terms. Credit: "U.S. Election Assistance Commission, Election Administration and Voting Survey".
    AP via CNN (2026)
    Unofficial results, as reported, from the results feed CNN publishes. Every 2026 figure is marked unofficial until the states certify.
    Vera Institute of Justice
    ICE Detention Trends, fiscal 2026 (average daily population by facility; the detention overlay). Published by Vera under its data access licence. Credit: Vera Institute of Justice.
    U.S. Department of Justice
    Press release 26-1310 (the Election Day monitoring list). Public domain unless otherwise indicated, as justice.gov states; citation is appreciated.
    House History, Art & Archives; Clerk of the House; National Archives; Senate
    Party divisions of the House and Senate, House statistics, electoral votes by year: counts, cited to each page in the files. These offices state no licence for them; the House's rights page covers images only, and none are used.
