Where the 2026 Senate vote moved, county by county

Against the 2024 presidential vote, the unofficial Senate results moved toward the Democrat in 2,156 of 2,177 counties in the 30 states where a Democrat ran against a Republican. The 21 that moved toward the Republican are in Maine, Illinois, Minnesota and Florida.
